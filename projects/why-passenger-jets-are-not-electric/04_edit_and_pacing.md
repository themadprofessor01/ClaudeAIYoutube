# Editing and Pacing Notes

## Principles
1. **One idea per shot, one number per beat.** Never show a number before the narrator says it, and never leave it after the next idea starts.
2. **Picture change every 3–5 seconds** (a cut, a reveal, a camera move with a new subject, or a new graphic). Holds longer than 6 s are reserved for the three big reveals: **50×**, **20×** and **120 t**.
3. **Cut on sound.** Bass hits, impacts and clicks are cut points. Every reveal has: *riser → cut → hit → silence → VO*.
4. **Silence is a tool.** The film's quietest moments (electric trainer, the stub battery column, "Twenty times.") are its most memorable. Don't fill them.
5. **Bookends.** The film opens and closes on the same golden-hour runway telephoto, and the Energy Lab returns three times (S4, S6.5, S7.3). Repetition = understanding.
6. **The persistent `20×` bug** (top-left, from 3:17 to 5:30) keeps the core number on screen while consequences unfold.

## Transition vocabulary (use only these)
- **Hard cut on a hit:** default for reveals.
- **Match cut:** shape rhymes (charge-port ring → engine intake; fuel column → map bar).
- **Light-flare wipe:** between chapters only.
- **Whiteout through hangar doors / sky:** location shifts.
- **Split-screen open/close:** the S4.16–S4.19 sequence only.
- **No** star wipes, glitch transitions, zoom-blur "TikTok" transitions or 3D page turns. They break the documentary tone.

## Speed and motion
- Exterior aircraft footage: real-time or subtle slow motion (≤ 50%) on touchdowns and rotations only.
- No speed ramps except the racer freeze (S2.3).
- Motion graphics ease: **expo-out** for appearances (fast in, gentle settle), **sine-in-out** for camera moves. Nothing bounces.

## Review checkpoints
1. **Animatic (grey-box) cut** with scratch VO: lock timing and shot order before any final rendering.
2. **Lighting/lookdev pass** on 5 hero frames: S1.2, S3.3, S4.6, S4.13, S5.5.
3. **Science review:** check every on-screen number against [`06_science_and_sources.md`](06_science_and_sources.md).
4. **Fine cut** with final VO and temp music.
5. **Sound mix + grade + final QC** on a phone screen and laptop speakers.
