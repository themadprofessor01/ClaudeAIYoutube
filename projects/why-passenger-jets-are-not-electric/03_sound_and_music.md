# Sound Design and Music

The sound must carry the same argument as the picture. **Amber/fuel has a sound** (warm, roaring, combustive, low-mid). **Cyan/battery has a sound** (clean electrical hum, tonal, high-mid, 120 Hz-based). When the two are compared, the viewer should be able to *hear* which is which with their eyes closed.

## Sonic palette

| Family | Sounds | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Jet / fuel (AMBER)** | Turbofan spool-up (starter whine → N1 rise), takeoff roar with LFE, fly-by Doppler, combustor rumble, exhaust shimmer, fuel slosh and pipe flow | Exteriors, engine cutaway, fuel columns |
| **Electric (CYAN)** | 50/60 Hz mains hum + harmonics, inverter whine (rising pitch with RPM), relay clicks, capacitor "charge" zips, transformer buzz, LED "tick" | Batteries, motors, charging, Energy Lab |
| **Mechanical / weight** | Heavy pallet impacts, forklift hydraulics, chain hoists, steel creaks, oleo strut compression, tyre squeal and smoke, structural groans | Battery stacking, overweight aircraft |
| **Interface / HUD** | Soft clicks, ticks, data chirps, counter rolls, map pings, a two-tone master-caution chime | Labels, counters, gauges |
| **Cinematic** | Deep whooshes, reverse swells, risers (incl. one Shepard-tone riser), sub drops, bass hits ("booms"), single piano/synth notes | Transitions and reveals |
| **Ambience** | Airport apron (APUs, tugs, distant PA, wind), hangar (huge reverb, fans), cabin rumble, high-altitude wind, birds at the grass airfield, sea | Beds per scene |

**Rule:** no comic or "cartoon" SFX. No boings, no whistles, no record scratches. Every sound should be something a real microphone could have captured, or a restrained cinematic element.

## Music: cue sheet

Style: modern, intelligent, cinematic, in the spirit of *pulse-driven hybrid score* (felt piano, analogue synth arps, low strings, processed percussion). Key centre **D minor → D major** at the hopeful turn. Tempo family 90 BPM (with half-time feel) so edits can hit beats.

| Cue | Time | Title | Description | Level under VO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | 0:29–1:15 | **"Departure"** | Drops in on the title hit. Low string drone + a ticking synth arp (16ths), felt piano motif (4 notes, rising). Thins out for the quiet electric-plane scenes. | −20 dB |
| M2 | 1:15–2:15 | **"Thrust"** | Builds: processed taiko-like percussion, a driving low-synth ostinato, swelling brass-like pads at the takeoff and climb. Cuts to **total silence** at 2:15. | −18 dB, peaks in gaps |
| M3 | 2:15–3:17 | **"Energy Lab"** | Minimal and clinical: a pulsing sine bass at 90 BPM, glassy plucks, lots of space. Bass hits at "50×" and "20×" are *part of the score* (tuned to D). | −22 dB (keep it airy) |
| M4 | 3:17–4:15 | **"The Weight"** | The Lab theme re-orchestrated heavier: low strings, slow-building drums. Split-screen section gets a stereo-wide pad. Ends on an unresolved chord at the heavy landing. | −20 dB |
| M5a | 4:15–5:30 | **"Spiral"** | Darkest cue: detuned low synths, a rhythmic pulse that **accelerates** during the weight spiral, a Shepard riser cut dead at "it never closes". | −20 dB |
| M5b | 5:31–6:45 | **"For Now"** | Hopeful turn: begins with a single warm pad, modulates to D major as the hangar doors open, adds light percussion and an uplifting piano melody (inversion of the M1 motif). | −20 dB |

Licensing: commission, or use a production-music library (Musicbed, Artlist, Epidemic, etc.) with stems so you can cut on the VO. Ask for **stems** (drums / bass / pads / melody) for every cue: Section 4 relies on muting layers on reveals.

## Section-by-section SFX design

### 1. Hook (0:00–0:30)
- **Cold open:** 4 s of pure sound before any VO. Airport ambience, then **engine spool-up** (starter whine → rising N1 tone → roar). Let the sub-bass build so viewers turn it up.
- Takeoff pass-by: full-bandwidth roar, **Doppler**, LFE rumble (20–60 Hz), grass and wind debris.
- EV montage: four tuned electrical "tick + hum" stabs, rising in pitch (a mini melody).
- "One brutal number": HUD click → electric swell → **metal groan** as the ghost aircraft sags → riser → **sub boom on the title**.

### 2. Electric planes exist (0:30–1:15)
- Deliberate *contrast*: after the jet roar, the electric trainer is **near-silent**: soft prop *whup*, birds, dew. Silence is the sound effect.
- Spec cards: soft HUD clicks per line.
- Racer: high electric scream + whoosh-by; speed-ramp freeze = low "thump" and a reverse tail.
- Solar plane: wind, faint motors; a **single piano note** on "one pilot".
- Hangar reveal: each light bank = big *CHUNK* (relay + metal + long reverb); escalating bass hits on "79 tonnes / 180 people / 800 km/h".

### 3. Energy demand (1:15–2:15)
- Fuel flowing in the X-ray: subtle liquid-in-pipe sound design.
- Takeoff HUD: speed-tape tick every 5 s, rising whine synced to speed.
- **Engine cutaway:** turbine drone, blade-pass shimmer (high metallic whirr), **filtered combustion roar** at the flame ring.
- "Small town": electrical surge, streetlights buzzing on in sequence (left-to-right panning).
- Climb: cloud "whoomph" through the layer; wind rush.
- 3,000 EV batteries: cascading relay clicks like dominoes, ending on a bass hit.
- ×7 for the 777: riser → **deep boom** → **hard silence** (≥1 s) into the Lab.

### 4. Jet fuel vs batteries (2:15–4:15) ★
Treat this like a product-film foley session: **close, clean, precise, expensive-sounding.**
- Lab lights: heavy theatrical clunks, long dark reverb (large-hall IR).
- Fuel cylinder set down: glass clink + liquid settle. Cells set down: dense metallic *clack*. Battery hum fades in, **only** while the battery is on screen.
- Scale settling: fine mechanical ticks, then **silence**.
- Fuel column (12 kWh): warm rising "burn" tone + whoosh that pans upward (use height/Atmos objects if delivering spatial audio), landing on a warm bass note.
- Battery column (0.25 kWh): short electric *zip* that **stops abruptly**. Let the silence be awkward.
- **"≈50×":** bass hit + sub drop (first big reveal).
- Heat loss: shimmering heat whoosh + roar fade. Motor: smooth rising inverter whine.
- Pack assembly: premium foley (snaps, slides, latch clicks, a connector "thunk").
- **"≈20×" stacking:** 20 soft electric ticks rising in pitch (tuned to the score) → **deep bass hit**. Then silence + one low note under "Twenty times."
- Fuel cube in the hangar: big room tone, liquid settle.
- **Battery pallets landing:** 20 heavy impacts, each with a floor-rumble tail; the electric hum **thickens with every pallet** (layer one more hum voice per pallet) into a riser. This is the most physical moment in the film; make it hit.
- Car comparison: four bass pulses on "one / cube / of / fuel."
- **Split-screen:** stereo separation, with left = amber engine tone, right = cyan hum. Left: falling "fuel drain" glide tone, engine pitch relaxes. Right: hum fades down as charge drains **but a low drone holds steady** (the weight that remains). Dry bass hit on "full one."
- Landings: left = light chirp; right = **heavy thud, long tyre squeal, metal stress groan**.

### 5. Breaks down (4:15–5:30)
- Hangar work ambience (impact wrench, rolling door).
- Map: radar-sweep tone on the holding pattern.
- "×20": riser → **biggest bass hit so far** on "120 tonnes" → pallets raining down.
- Overweight: hydraulic strain, creaking structure, tyre bulge squeak, **two-tone master-caution chime** (realistic aviation-style, not a game beep), sub rumble.
- Weight spiral: accelerating pulse + **Shepard-tone riser**, cut dead.
- Takeoff heat: electrical buzz intensifying, pump whirr, thermal ticking.
- Empty cabin: eerie hum, faint electrical whine; range ring "deflates" (descending glide).
- 777 mountain: building rumble → **final sub boom** → 1 s of silence.

### 6. Future (5:30–6:45)
- Single cyan light = soft electrical click.
- Hangar doors: deep rolling rumble → music modulates to major.
- Meridian: **emphasise quiet**, with a gentle multi-prop whir and birds and sea underneath. This is a selling point.
- Hybrid: layered hum + soft turbine whine.
- Battery history row: rising chime per cell, building upward.
- Threshold line: riser that **resolves** (the first riser in the film that doesn't end in a boom).
- SAF plant: industrial ambience. Hydrogen tank: **cryogenic hiss**, frosting crackle.

### 7. Conclusion (6:45–7:25)
- Mirror the opening: runway ambience + engine idle → takeoff roar fades quickly under the swelling music.
- Electric motor macro: smooth whine.
- Recap: Lab bass hit returns on "twenty times."
- Final line: music drops on "kilogram". One sustained low note, then a distant jet passing far overhead as the screen goes black.

## Mix and delivery spec

| Item | Target |
|---|---|
| Integrated loudness | **−14 LUFS** (YouTube), true peak ≤ **−1 dBTP** |
| Dialogue | Anchored around −16 to −18 LUFS short-term; always intelligible. VO wins every conflict. |
| Music under VO | Ducked 18–22 dB below the voice (sidechain + manual rides); swells only in VO gaps |
| SFX under VO | Keep transients short; sustained beds (engine roar, hum) **EQ-carved 1–4 kHz** to leave room for the voice |
| LFE / sub | Plenty below 60 Hz for the jet and pallet moments, but high-pass the music bass at 30 Hz and check on phone speakers (the "boom" needs a 100–200 Hz component to read on small speakers) |
| Silence | Intentional silences ≥ 0.8 s at: 0:11 ("Even trucks."), 2:15, 2:41, 3:17, 4:33, 5:30 |
| Stems | Deliver VO / Music / SFX / Ambience stems + a full mix. Also an M&E mix (no VO) for localisation/dubbing. |
| Reference check | Mix on studio monitors, then verify on laptop speakers, phone speaker and earbuds |
