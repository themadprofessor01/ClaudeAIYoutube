**4:18** → S5.2

**4:31** → S5.3

**4:35** → S5.4

**4:38** → S5.5

**4:47** → S5.6

**5:02** → S5.7

**5:11** → S5.8

**5:22** → S5.9

## 6. WHAT COULD WORK IN THE FUTURE (5:30–6:45)

**5:31** → S6.1

**5:39** → S6.2

**5:47** → S6.3

**5:59** → S6.4

**6:08** → S6.5

**6:22** → S6.6

**6:31** → S6.7
And for the long haul, / the answer may not be batteries at all, / but **sustainable aviation fuels**, / or **hydrogen**. / Each with hard challenges of its own.
