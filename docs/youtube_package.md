The answer isn't in your skin. It's a hidden prediction system in your brain that guesses what your own movements will feel like and turns the volume down. In this video we follow the science from a 1971 foot-tickling study to brain scanners and tickle robots. Along the way: crickets that turn down their own hearing, rats that seem to laugh, and why "you hit me harder!" might actually be true.
